Who can use it

The app is for DCU students. You need a DCU email address that you control. Keep your password to yourself. You are responsible for what is posted from your account.

What you may post

Deadlines and class reports are seen by other students on your modules. There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Do not post anything that:

  • is abusive, harassing, hateful, threatening, sexual or violent;
  • names, targets or reveals personal information about anyone;
  • is spam, advertising, or deliberately false, such as a made-up deadline or a class reported cancelled when it wasn't;
  • breaks the law or DCU's student code of conduct.

Reporting and moderation

Every deadline has a menu with Report and "Hide posts from this person". Reports are reviewed within 24 hours. Content that breaks these terms is removed. The account that posted it may be banned, temporarily or permanently. A deadline reported by several students is hidden until it has been reviewed.

Accuracy

Timetables come from DCU's published timetable. Deadlines and reports come from other students. Any of them can be wrong or out of date, so for anything that matters, such as exams and submission dates, check with DCU, your lecturer or Loop.

Ending your account

You can delete your account in the app at any time. An account that breaks these terms may be suspended or closed.
