DCU Timetable — tutorial text
==============================

Edit anything below the dashed lines. Each step has a TITLE (one bold line) and a BODY
(one or two short sentences). Keep titles under about 30 characters and bodies under
about 140, so the card fits on a small phone with large text.

To drop a step, delete its whole block. To add one, copy a block and say in POINTS AT
what it should highlight. Button labels can be changed too.

"POINTS AT" is for me — it says what the cut-out goes around. Change it if you want a
step to highlight something else.
"ACTION" is an animated feature to suggest a movement the user should make to progress steps.
Every control a step points at, the student presses themselves: those steps have no Next.

The order runs forward through the app so the student rarely has to come back:
day → week → a class (and back) → Deadlines → More, which ends on the week.


PHONE AND WEB
=============

Step 1 — Welcome
POINTS AT: nothing (a card in the middle of the screen)
--------------------------------------------------------
TITLE: Welcome to DCU Timetable
BODY: Here's a quick tour of the basics.
BUTTONS: Start | Skip


Step 2 — Your day
POINTS AT: the day's list of classes
--------------------------------------------------------
TITLE: Your day
BODY: View each day's modules here. See locations, class type and lecturers.
BUTTONS: Next


Step 3 — Other days
POINTS AT: the day's heading ("Wednesday")
--------------------------------------------------------
TITLE: Swipe left to see tomorrow
ACTION: Animated swiping feature (Next also works: a swipe isn't a button)


Step 4 — The week
POINTS AT: the week / day button, top left
--------------------------------------------------------
TITLE: See the whole week
BODY: Tap here to switch to the week grid.   (drafted by Claude — edit freely)
ACTION: Tap the button; the timetable switches to the week grid


Step 5 — A class (on the week grid)
POINTS AT: the week's first class in the grid
--------------------------------------------------------
TITLE: Tap a class
BODY: See its room, lecturer and what's due — and report it cancelled so your classmates know.
ACTION: Tap the class to open it


Step 5b — Report cancelled (on the class's page; counts as part of step 5)
POINTS AT: the "Report: lecture cancelled" button, once it's scrolled into view
           ("Mark as cancelled" for a moderator)
--------------------------------------------------------
TITLE: Swipe down to see report cancelled
ACTION: Animated swipe up the page, until the button is on screen; then tap it and confirm
Then, once it's in view (drafted by Claude — edit freely):
TITLE: Report it cancelled
BODY: Tap here to report it. It's a practice run — nobody else sees it.
(A practice: the report stays on this phone and is never sent.)


Step 5c — Take it back (counts as part of step 5)
POINTS AT: the "Undo my report" button ("Mark as on" for a moderator)
--------------------------------------------------------
TITLE: Now take it back   (drafted by Claude — edit freely)
BODY: Tap here to undo it. A report can always be undone.   (drafted by Claude — edit freely)
ACTION: Tap the button


Step 5d — Back to the week (counts as part of step 5)
POINTS AT: the Back button, top left of the class's page
--------------------------------------------------------
TITLE: Go back   (drafted by Claude — edit freely)
BODY: Tap Back, at the top left, to return to your week.   (drafted by Claude — edit freely)
ACTION: Tap Back


Step 6 — Deadlines
POINTS AT: the Deadlines tab at the bottom
--------------------------------------------------------
TITLE: Deadlines
BODY: Tap here to open them.   (drafted by Claude — edit freely)
ACTION: Tap the tab


Step 6b — On the Deadlines page (counts as part of step 6)
POINTS AT: nothing (a card over the Deadlines page)
--------------------------------------------------------
TITLE: Deadlines
BODY: Assignments and tests shared by your classmates. Add one and everyone on the module sees it.
BUTTONS: Next


Step 7 — More
(The tour brings the student back to the timetable first: ⋯ is only there.)
POINTS AT: the ⋯ button, top right
--------------------------------------------------------
TITLE: More
BODY: Edit your timetable, set class alerts, export it to your calendar, and manage your account.
ACTION: Tap ⋯; the menu opens, and the tour waits for it to close


Step 8 — Thank you!
(Back on the timetable, in the week grid the student switched to)
POINTS AT: nothing (a card in the middle of the screen)
--------------------------------------------------------
TITLE: Thank you!
BODY: You're all set. Replay this tour any time from Account.   (drafted by Claude — edit freely)
BUTTONS: Done
